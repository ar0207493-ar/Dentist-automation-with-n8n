A more comfortable first dental visit starts before you arrive

A little preparation, a few questions and room to tell the team what would help you feel at ease.

Know the practical details

Before the appointment, ask the clinic where to arrive, how long to allow and what information to bring. Confirm the purpose of the visit so you know whether it is an initial conversation, an examination or planned treatment.

Prepare a list of medicines and relevant health information if the team requests it. Previous records may also be useful; the clinic can explain whether they are needed.

Tell the team what would help

You do not need to hide a difficult previous experience or uncertainty about treatment. Say if you would prefer each step explained, need a moment to ask questions or would like to discuss comfort before anything begins.

A first conversation can focus on what matters to you. The team can then explain the assessment and discuss a plan with those details in mind.

Write down the questions you most want answered. Bring the list into the room with you.

Make space for the decision

If treatment is proposed, ask what it involves, whether there are alternatives and what the cost and follow-up may be. It is useful to understand the plan in language that feels clear to you.

Before you leave, check the next step and how to raise any questions you think of afterwards. You can bring notes to make the conversation easier to remember.
