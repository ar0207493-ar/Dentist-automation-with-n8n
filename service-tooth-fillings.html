Small details.
A considered decision.

If a filling is recommended, ask your dentist to explain the area being treated and the options available. Your tooth, bite, preferences and the extent of treatment all belong in the conversation.

Discuss the material, the steps involved and the expected cost before treatment. Existing dental work also needs ongoing care, so ask how to look after it and what changes to report.

What to expect

A clear path through your visit.

  1. 01Assess the tooth.

    Explain your symptoms and let your dentist assess the tooth before deciding on treatment.

  2. 02Choose with understanding.

    Talk through suitable materials, alternatives, comfort measures and costs.

  3. 03Review your aftercare.

    Ask what to expect, what to do if your bite feels different and when to seek advice.

Make room for your questions

Ask the things
that matter to you.

You deserve to understand the recommendation before choosing what happens next. These questions can help start the conversation.

  • Why is a filling recommended for this tooth?
  • Which materials or other options are suitable?
  • How should I care for the restoration afterwards?

Before your visit.

A little preparation can make the next conversation feel easier.

All your questions, answered
Can I choose a tooth-coloured filling?

Ask your dentist which materials are suitable for the tooth and the treatment involved. Suitability is determined after assessment.

How long will a filling last?

No restoration lasts forever. Its condition, your bite and your daily care all matter. Your dentist can review existing fillings at checkups.

What if I already have a filling that feels different?

Tell a dentist about a new change in comfort, your bite or the filling itself. An examination can help determine the next step.
