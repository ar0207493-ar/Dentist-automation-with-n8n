## Animation

GSAP 3.15.0, ScrollTrigger, SplitText, and DrawSVGPlugin, downloaded from the official GSAP npm distribution via jsDelivr and hosted locally. Their original license headers are retained in `dist/assets/vendor/`. Documentation and licensing: https://gsap.com/docs/v3/Installation/ and https://gsap.com/standard-license/

Lenis 1.3.27, hosted locally from the official npm distribution via jsDelivr. Its MIT license is retained in `dist/assets/vendor/lenis-LICENSE.txt`. Source and documentation: https://github.com/darkroomengineering/lenis

## Type

DM Sans, DM Serif Display, Inter, Jost, and Manrope, served through Google Fonts. The header wordmark uses outlines derived from Jost SemiBold.

## Smile comparison

`dist/assets/reference/smile-comparison.png` is an AI-generated illustrative whitening comparison, created with the built-in image generation tool. It is labeled as an illustration on the page.

## Social icons

Facebook, Instagram, X and YouTube SVG paths are from [Simple Icons](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons), distributed under [CC0](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/develop/LICENSE.md). Local source SVGs are in `dist/assets/icons/`; shared footer markup inlines the paths for theme-aware styling. Brand trademarks remain with their owners.

## WebP delivery

Raster photographs and the illustrative smile comparison are served as optimized WebP copies of the credited source assets. Original credits and licenses above continue to apply. The 22 delivered raster assets total approximately 1.72 MB, compared with 5.07 MB for their JPEG/PNG sources.

## Self-hosted fonts

DM Serif Display and Jost Latin WOFF2 subsets are served locally from `dist/assets/fonts/`. They are distributed under the SIL Open Font License; the original OFL notices are included beside the font files. Source: Google Fonts (`google/fonts`), with subsets delivered by `fonts.gstatic.com`.
